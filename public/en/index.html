FAQ

Do I need to install anything or sign up?

No install. Design, preview and download in your browser; images are processed locally and not uploaded; only when you press Share yourself is that one share picture made public. Looking at the sample needs no login. To add your own images, sign in with Google in one click; there is no separate account to create.

What 3D printer do I need?

An FDM printer that supports multi-material printing, and a slicer that opens 3MF files. Match colors to the filament you already have.

Can I use IKEA SKÅDIS accessories? Are other hole patterns coming?

The current staggered-slot panels have been tested with original IKEA SKÅDIS accessories. Other hole patterns are under consideration. Tell us which you would like through feature requests; released features will appear in the updates.

How big can it be?

Set each panel’s width and height from 40 to 240 mm in 20 mm steps, then mix them into an L-shape, stepped layout or larger wall. Panels measuring 120, 160, 200 or 240 mm share the same hole pattern.

Will I have to pay again later?

No. It is a one-time purchase with no subscription, and every future update to the web app is included, so you always have the latest version. If we ever shut down the online service, we will provide an offline version so you can keep using it.
