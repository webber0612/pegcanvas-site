FAQ

Do I need to install anything or sign up?

No install. Design, preview and download in your browser; images are processed locally and never uploaded. The current preview needs no login. The full release uses one-click Google sign-in, with no separate account to create.

What 3D printer do I need?

An FDM printer that supports multi-material printing, and a slicer that opens 3MF files. Match colors to the filament you already have.

How big can it be?

Each panel can be 40–240 mm in 20 mm steps; 120, 160, 200 and 240 mm keep the hole pattern identical on every panel. Tile panels in any direction to cover a whole wall.

Will I have to pay again later?

No. It is a one-time purchase with no subscription, and every future update to the web app is included, so you always have the latest version. If we ever shut down the online service, we will provide an offline version so you can keep using it.
