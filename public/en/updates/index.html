NEW

  • Smart filament match: pick a material and your brands, and one press finds the nearest real filaments. 57 brands, over three thousand colors.
  • A numbered filament list, in the order your slicer uses.
  • One-press sharing, with share pictures in six looks.

IMPROVED

  • Pictures with many colors no longer freeze the page.

10 October 2026

NEW

  • A photo of your wall: add one, mark a length you know, and preview the design at true scale.
  • Align and snap: align buttons, snapping while you drag or resize, and turning an image from outside its corners.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys move an image, Delete removes an image or a panel.
  • Support chat: an AI assistant answers first and hands the rest to a person.
  • Accounts and plans: sign in with Google and try paid features first.
  • The public beta begins.

IMPROVED

  • An image dragged off the wall leaves an outline you can click to get it back.
  • The canvas shows each panel's dimensions.
  • Parts no longer leave the plate when flipped or rotated in Bambu Studio.

9 October 2026

NEW

  • Panels of any size: 120, 160, 200 or 240 mm, mixed freely.
  • Installation drawing and panel map: A4 pictures showing every screw hole and where each panel goes.

  • Large designs: over 36 plates are split into several 3MF files.
  • Smarter image import: a suggested color count, near-duplicate colors merged, specks removed, the background detected.
  • A phone layout, and saving to Google Drive.
