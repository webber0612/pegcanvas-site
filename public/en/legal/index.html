Privacy notice

PegCanvas is owned and operated by Babyglider Online Store, a company registered in the Philippines. Questions or requests about this notice go to hello@pegcanvas.com.

Your images and designs

The editor at app.pegcanvas.com runs in your browser. The images you add, your designs and the print files you generate are processed on your own device and are not uploaded to us. Work in progress is kept in your browser's storage on that device so you can pick it up again; clearing your browser data removes it. Project files and print files are saved only where you choose to download them.

What we receive

  • Email you send us. If you write to us, for example to be told when paid plans open, we keep your email address and message to reply and to send that notice. Ask at any time and we will delete them.
  • Support conversations. When you use “Contact support” on our pages, the email address you give and the conversation are stored by our support service (hosted on Cloudflare) and passed to a messaging tool used internally by our team so we can answer. Conversations are deleted automatically after 90 days; write to us to have one deleted sooner. Common questions are answered first by an AI assistant, and those answers are labelled; the rest are answered by our team, who may also use an AI assistant to help write replies. To produce an answer, the text of the conversation is sent to Mancer, an AI service provider, without your email address. Please do not share passwords or payment details in the conversation. Your browser remembers an identifier for the conversation so you can see replies when you return; clearing your browser data removes it.
  • Technical logs. Our pages are delivered by Cloudflare, which handles your IP address, browser type and the pages requested in order to serve the site and protect it from abuse.
  • Visit statistics. pegcanvas.com uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. It works without cookies and does not identify individual visitors.
  • Fonts. pegcanvas.com loads its display typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers when you open the site.
  • Language choice. Your language preference is stored in your browser, not on our servers.

We do not run advertising, we do not track you across sites, we do not use tracking cookies, and we do not sell personal information.

Signing in with Google

The editor works without an account. Signing in with Google is optional and unlocks more features. Nothing is sent to Google until you open the sign-in window.

  • What we read. Your name, email address and profile picture, from the sign-in Google returns to your browser. They are kept in that browser only, to show that you are signed in. We do not store them on a server, and signing out removes them.
  • Google Drive. If you choose to save a project to Google Drive, the editor asks for permission to see and manage only the files it creates there. It cannot see anything else in your Drive. Project files go straight from your browser to your own Drive; they do not pass through us, and you can delete them or withdraw the permission in your Google Account at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, or to correct or delete it, by writing to the address above. If this notice changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
