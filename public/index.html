From image to object

Design first.
Print your way.

  1. 01

    Bring your artwork

    Import PNG, JPEG or SVG. Compare reduced-color previews before adding an image to your layout, while keeping the original source in your project.

  2. 02

    Make it fit your space

    Arrange image layers across modular panels. Move, scale and rotate artwork, then customize the palette for each panel.

  3. 03

    Take it to your slicer

    Preview the generated geometry and export multicolor 3MF files. Save a reusable PegCanvas project to return to your design later.

Pre-launch development

The current prototype processes images and generates models locally in the browser. The product is being developed around hands-on experience running a 3D-printed toy business.

We are refining the editor and validating exported files and physical prints before crowdfunding. Public access and pricing will be announced when ready.
